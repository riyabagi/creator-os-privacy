Privacy Policy – Creator OS

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Creator OS. Creator OS is an Instagram content management platform designed to help creators manage their Instagram account information, content performance, engagement metrics, and creator activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use Creator OS.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use and permissions you grant, Creator OS may access or process the following information:

  • Instagram account ID and username.
  • Instagram profile information.
  • Instagram media and content information.
  • Engagement metrics and account insights, where permitted.
  • Authentication tokens required to connect your Instagram account.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information is used to:

  • Connect and display Instagram account information.
  • Track Instagram content performance.
  • Display engagement metrics and available insights.
  • Manage content and creator activities.
  • Maintain and improve Creator OS functionality.

4. Data Storage and Security

Information processed by Creator OS may be stored in its configured application database. Reasonable security measures are intended to protect information and authentication credentials against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

5. Sharing of Information

Creator OS does not sell personal information. Information may be processed through Meta services when connecting to Instagram and through infrastructure or service providers used to operate the application.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as needed for the application's functionality and applicable requirements.

Users can request deletion of their personal information and connected Instagram account data by contacting us using the email address below. Requests will be reviewed and handled subject to applicable legal and platform requirements.

7. Third-Party Services

Creator OS integrates with Instagram services provided by Meta. Your use of Instagram is also subject to Meta's applicable terms, policies, and privacy practices.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in application functionality or data practices. Updates will be published on this page.
